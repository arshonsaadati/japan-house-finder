# Per-town minpaku ordinance checklist

**This file is the gate before any offer.** A cheap house in a town that bans
host-absent minpaku is worthless for this plan. The scraper flags any listing
in a town not marked `verified: yes` below.

Legend for "Host-absent minpaku OK?": **yes** / **no** / **restricted** (zones
or seasons) / **UNVERIFIED** (assumption pending a call to the town office).

build (2026-08-29) **all rows are UNVERIFIED** — they carry the handoff's
priors only. Verifying them is the top follow-up task (see Open questions).

## How to verify a row

1. Call/email the town's 住宅宿泊事業 (minpaku) desk — usually under 環境課 or 商工観光課.
2. Ask specifically: (a) can a **non-resident, host-absent** operator run a
   minpaku here, (b) are there zones/seasons where it's prohibited (post-2026-07-15
   national guidance lets towns declare zero-day zones), (c) any local notification
   requirements beyond the national 届出.
3. Get the name of at least one registered 住宅宿泊管理業者 (management company)
   that will take a property here — **legally required for a non-resident owner**,
   and availability varies a lot by town.
4. Fill the row, set `Verified: yes`, cite the source + date.
